Distribution Judgment Task

How to play:

  • In each round, you will see two series of numbers, one in each box.
  • The numbers are past results of two slot machines in a casino.
  • One machine gives higher results on average.
  • After the last number appears, click the machine you think has the higher average.
  • You will start with 5 practice rounds.
