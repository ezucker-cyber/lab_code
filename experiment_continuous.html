Instructions

  • Two boxes will show numbers. One box tends to draw from a higher distribution than the other — but this may change during the sequence.
  • After each number appears, adjust the scale to reflect your current belief about which box is drawing from the higher distribution. Centre = 50/50. Sliding right means you think the right box is higher.
  • The scale has 10% steps from 0 to 100.
  • When the sequence ends, adjust the scale one final time and click Confirm choice.
