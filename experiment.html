Distribution Judgment Task

How to play:

  • On each round, two boxes take turns showing you numbers. One box will tend to show higher numbers than the other — but it may switch throughout the trial.
  • Your goal is to track which box is higher by the end of the sequence, not which one started out higher.
  • After the last number appears, click the box you think finished higher.
